## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, tactile lifestyle aesthetic crafted for holistic self-care, intuitive nutrition, home cooking, and mindful fitness. Rejecting the sterile, clinical metrics common in athletic trackers, the interface behaves as a warm culinary journal and personal companion.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Warm & Tactile:** Earthy, sun-drenched undertones replace cold digital slates. The UI evokes heavy woven paper, ceramic dinnerware, and sunlit terracotta countertops.
- **Supportive & Grounded:** Interacting with the system feels nourishing and forgiving, never punitive. Data displays celebrate nourishment over restriction.
- **Intimate Editorial:** Clean grotesque letterforms and warm, generous spacing produce an inviting, reflective rhythm akin to an artisanal food and living quarterly.

### Voice & Copywriting Tone
The voice addresses the user directly in an informal, caring French register (*tutoiement*), anchoring everyday routines in self-worth rather than clinical achievement ("Prends soin de ta valeur"). Microcopy is conversational, calm, and reassuring.

## Colors

The palette centers on earth pigments, organic botanicals, and sun-baked ceramics. High contrast is maintained against cream-sand backdrops to preserve readability under ambient daylight.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#D97736` Terracotta Amber):** Primary touchpoints, completed actions, active tabs, energetic milestones, and total energy/calorie indicators.
- **Secondary (`#587053` Warm Olive / Sage):** Fitness intervals, botanical meal tags, mindful rest markers, and lipid/healthy fat nutrition indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D4943C` Golden Ochre):** Culinary accents, recipe tags, harvest highlights, and carbohydrate indicators.
- **Neutral Dark (`#29231F` Roasted Cacao):** Primary text and structural icon fills, offering soft high contrast without the harshness of pure black.

### Supporting Semantic & Macro Indicators
- **Proteins:** `#CF6B5A` (Earthy Rose Coral)
- **Carbohydrates:** `#D4943C` (Golden Ochre)
- **Lipids / Healthy Fats:** `#587053` (Warm Olive)
- **Fibers & Botanicals:** `#3F7A74` (Soft Mediterranean Teal)
- **Base Canvas:** `#FAF7F2` (Unbleached Sand Linen)
- **Card Surface Tier 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Bone Porcelain)
- **Card Surface Tier 2:** `#F3EDE2` (Oatmeal Cream)
- **Subtle Hairlines:** `#E5DCCD` (Sun-bleached Cord)

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites the expressive, sculptural rhythm of **Epilogue** with the friendly grotesque balance of **Karla**.

### Roles & Composition
- **Epilogue (Headlines & Editorial Callouts):** Adds deliberate personality, warmth, and visual weight. Used for hero titles, section headings, and meal names. Set with snug negative tracking on larger sizes to evoke high-end food periodicals.
- **Karla (Body & Interface Details):** Handles long-form journaling, recipe ingredient counts, preparation instructions, and compact nutrient badges. Its open aperture and lively terminal geometry retain exceptional legibility at small sizes while avoiding corporate sterility.
- **Nutritional & Numeric Data:** Metrics paired with macro markers leverage `label-lg` or `headline-sm` with tight line heights to maintain tight vertical cohesion within circular progress bars and modular daily intake rings.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts balance compact mobile usability with the airy breathing room of a lifestyle journal. 

### Grid Philosophy
- **Mobile (1 to 4 Columns):** Single fluid column stack with 20px (`1.25rem`) side gutters. Sticky bottom navigation and thumb-friendly meal creation triggers adhere to safe area margins.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Two balanced panes separating the meal preparation timeline/journaling stream on the left from daily macros, fitness summaries, and pantry stock on the right.
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Constrained container (max-width 1240px) allowing expansive recipe editorial layouts, multi-day calendar overviews, and deep-dive weekly wellness analytics.

### Rhythm & Density
Whitespace functions as an active calming agent. Component internal padding leans generous (`space-lg` across primary cards), preventing metric fatigue and emphasizing artisanal food imagery. Touch targets strictly respect an accessible minimum dimension of 44x44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering and soft, diffused ambient light rather than stark drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`):** Ground level. The warm unbleached paper surface on which cards rest.
- **Tonal Tier 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary interactive containers, recipes, and diary entries. Soft diffused shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(41, 35, 31, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(41, 35, 31, 0.03)`.
- **Tonal Tier 2 (`#F3EDE2`):** Recessed micro-containers (macro summary pods, ingredient check-boxes, pantry shelves) framed with a subtle interior hairline (`#E5DCCD`).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Elevated navigation docks, bottom sheets, and timers. Ambient shadow: `0 18px 40px -8px rgba(41, 35, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(41, 35, 31, 0.04)`.

Shadows are exclusively cast with a warm roast brown tint (`rgba(41, 35, 31, ...)`) to ensure depth feels natural and sunny.

## Shapes

The design system adopts pill and organic curved contours (Roundedness scale level 3).

### Shape Application
- **Buttons, Badges, Macro Pills:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide tactile comfort and invite spontaneous finger taps.
- **Cards & Modules (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`):** Primary meal journals and recipe cards employ 2rem to 3rem radii, mimicking smooth beach stones and hand-thrown ceramic platters.
- **Media Containers:** Recipe photos and workout video covers carry 2rem corner curves with soft internal edge borders to prevent harsh perimeter contrasts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill button, solid Terracotta `#D97736` fill, cream text (`#FAF7F2`), subtle hover/active scale down (`0.98`), 48px minimum height.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Cream surface (`#F3EDE2`) with `#29231F` text. Emphasizes optional flows such as "Ajouter une note" or "Remplacer l'ingrédient".
- **Floating Action Button (Meal & Mood Log):** Circular 56x56px pill button with prominent terracotta warmth and soft ambient shadow, fixed comfortably in the lower right or center dock.

### Chips & Macro Tags
- **Macro Badges:** Pill-shaped pill capsules (height 28px) displaying key numbers alongside nutrient tints:
  - *Calories:* `#D97736` with 12% tint background.
  - *Protéines:* `#CF6B5A` with 12% tint background.
  - *Glucides:* `#D4943C` with 12% tint background.
  - *Lipides:* `#587053` with 12% tint background.
  - *Fibres:* `#3F7A74` with 12% tint background.
- **Filter Chips:** 36px height, rounded full, subtle 1px border `#E5DCCD` when inactive; solid `#587053` with white text when selected.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed rounded-pill or `rounded-2xl` text fields with `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EDE2` surfaces, 48px height, padded with 16px horizontally.
- Subtle inner hairline border `#E5DCCD`. Focus state transitions to a 1.5px `#D97736` ring with zero abrupt motion.

### Cards & Feed Tiles
- **Recipe & Meal Log Cards:** Generous `rounded-3xl` (32px) container, pure white surface `#FFFFFF`, 20px padding. Imagery fills the top perimeter with seamless corner radii. Macro summaries sit clustered at the bottom edge in compact, easily scannable pills.
- **Pantry / Fridge Item Row:** Modular horizontal card with `rounded-2xl` (20px), soft tactile check-off toggle, item shelf-life badge, and tap-to-replenish control.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes (Meal Planning & Grocery):** 24x24px rounded circles (`border-radius: 50%`) rather than harsh squares, filling with warm sage `#587053` and displaying a crisp cream checkmark upon completion.
- **Segmented Time Tabs:** Pill container in `#F3EDE2` featuring an animated sliding white pill thumb with subtle elevation to switch between "Matin", "Midi", "Soir", and "En-cas".
